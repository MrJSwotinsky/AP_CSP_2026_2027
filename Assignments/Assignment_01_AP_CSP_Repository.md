# Assignment 1 - AP CSP Repository
Due: Tuesday, November 3rd 2026

## Sign Up for GitHub
1) [Click Here](https://www.github.com) to navigate to GitHub.
2) Click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the screen.
3) For **email** enter your NYC Public Schools email address.
4) For **password** create a secure password.
5) For **username** enter `LastNameFirstInitial-IEC` (e.g. `SwotinskyJ-IEC`)
6) Leave the box labeled "Receive occasional product updates and announcements" unchecked.
7) Click the green "Create Account" button.
8) An 8-digit code will be sent to your email.  Enter that code to verify your email address.
9) Sign in to your new GitHub account.

## Create Your APCSP Portfolio Repository (a.k.a Repo)
1) Click your avatar in the top right corner of the screen (Your avatar will look like a pixelated image in a small circle).
2) From the dropdown menu, click "Profile".
3) From the top menu, click the "Repositories" tab.
4) Click the green "New" button.
5) Name your repo `LastNameFirstInitial-APCSP-Portfolio` (e.g. `SwotinskyJ-APCSP-Portfolio`)
6) Set the visibility of your repo to "Private".
7) Set create README.md file to "On"
8) Set add license to "Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal"
9) Click the green "Create Repository" button.

## Share Your APCSP Portfolio Repo
1) Open your `APCSP-Portfolio` repo.
2) From the top menu, click the "Settings" button.
3) From the left menu bar, click "Collaborators".
4) In the center of the screen, click "Add People".
5) Enter `MrJSwotinsky`.
6) Click the green "Add MrJSwotinsky" button.

### Congratulations...You have now created and shared your first Repo!
